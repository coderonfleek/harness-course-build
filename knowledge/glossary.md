
# Internal Glossary

- **RSVP** — Routing Service Verification Protocol. Our internal
  handshake between the routing engine and warehouse coordination
  service to confirm a route is loadable before dispatch.
- **Cold-shelf** — A package that has been in a warehouse longer than
  72 hours without a routing decision. Triggers an automated escalation.
- **The Grid** — Slang name for the internal ops dashboard. Officially
  called the Client Coordination Console.
- **Kaboom** — Nickname for the deprecated V1 billing service.
  Fully decommissioned in 2023 but the term persists.
- **RFR** — Routing Failure Report. Auto-generated when a package
  fails to route within its SLA window.
